Assignment reflection

What I learned from building this blog.

Creating this blog helped me understand that blogging is not only about writing text. It also requires planning, design, content organization, SEO, and an understanding of the target audience. Before this assignment, I did not think much about keywords, meta descriptions, headings, and how they affect the visibility and readability of online content.

One of the main challenges was choosing topics that were connected to each other while still being interesting as separate articles. I also had to organize the information clearly and make each blog post easy to read. Selecting a suitable visual style and creating a consistent design across desktop and mobile screens was another important part of the process.

I believe blogging can support my future career because it gives me a place to demonstrate my knowledge, projects, and communication skills. A professional blog can also work as part of an online portfolio. In the future, I can continue publishing articles about technology, programming, artificial intelligence, and projects that I complete during my studies.
